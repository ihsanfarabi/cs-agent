# Billing

## Invoices

Invoices are issued on the first day of each calendar month. Each invoice covers the previous month's subscription and usage.

## Overages

Usage beyond plan limits is invoiced at the end of the billing cycle. Overage charges appear as a separate line on the next invoice.

## Refunds

Request a refund within 14 days of the invoice date. Refunds are issued to the original invoice.
